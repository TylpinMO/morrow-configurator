# Morrow Configurator

Morrow Configurator is an editorial storefront concept for a modular task lamp. It turns product configuration into the central interaction: every finish, arm, light-temperature, and mount choice updates the product study, specifications, and price.

## Highlights

- Four finish systems with a CSS-rendered product study
- Compact and tall arm configurations
- Three light temperatures and two mounting options
- Front, side, and detail product views
- Functional bag with quantity controls and live totals
- Responsive layouts and accessible keyboard focus states
- No stock product imagery or checkout pretence

## Stack

React 19, TypeScript, Next-compatible routing through Vinext, and CSS.
